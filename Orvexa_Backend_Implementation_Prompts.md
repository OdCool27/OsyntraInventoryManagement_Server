# Orvexa — Backend Implementation Prompts (Spring Boot / PostgreSQL)

**Usage:** Give Codex the common instructions and **one numbered phase at a time**. Ask it to inspect existing implementation and present a brief plan before editing. Advance only after reviewing tests and behavior. Adapt class/package names to the existing project.

## Project context and non-negotiable instructions
You are implementing **Osyntra Orvexa**, a cloud-first, multi-tenant retail, inventory and POS SaaS for Jamaican businesses, with a path to internationalization. This is an implementation plan for an existing codebase, **not permission to replace it**. Inspect the repository, current README, package/build files, existing architecture, entities, routes, and conventions before changing code. Preserve working functionality, names and existing design patterns unless changes are justified and explained. Do not create parallel duplicate services or speculative infrastructure. Use clear, simple comments for nontrivial business logic, concurrency, security and calculations; keep the README accurate. Use incremental migrations, small cohesive changes, and avoid unnecessary dependencies. No hardcoded credentials. For each phase: inspect, outline impacted files and data migrations, implement, test, update README, then report completed work, limitations and exact next steps. **Do not implement every phase in one run.** Stop at the end of the requested phase for review. Where a feature is already implemented, extend it rather than rebuilding it. Keep product name **Orvexa** and company **Osyntra** consistent. Treat this document as requirements, not a claim that all functionality already exists.

### Confirmed product decisions
- Cloud-only online POS for first release; block unsafe checkout during connectivity failures and reconcile ambiguous payment outcomes before retrying. Offline POS is deferred.
- Tenant-wide configuration with controlled location overrides for POS, tax where legally appropriate, branding, notifications and prices. Separate location inventories and weighted-average costing **per location**.
- Identity: one user account may belong to multiple businesses; each membership has a fixed role (Owner, Administrator, Manager, Cashier, Inventory Clerk) and permitted branches. Secure email invitations, expiring single-use tokens, existing-user acceptance, and business switcher. Platform staff have a separate Osyntra administration role model.
- Jamaica-guided tax profiles, configurable and versioned, with nonregistered-business support, zero-rated/exempt distinctions, historical tax snapshots and clear owner verification. Never assume every item is subject to 15% GCT. Treat imported-goods costs separately from sales tax. Confirm actual rules with TAJ/accountants before production.
- Central catalogue with simple products and parent/variant products; variant-level SKU, barcode, price and stock; global per-product low-stock threshold applied independently to each location. Optional batches and expiry dates with alerts and backend sale restrictions. Multiple purchasing/selling units with explicit base-unit conversions.
- Manufacturer barcodes plus **internal** barcode generation and printable labels; internal codes are not represented as GS1-registered GTINs.
- Guided CSV/XLSX product import with templates, mapping, validation, previews, duplicates and recoverable import status; illustrated, resumable progressive onboarding without blocking dashboard access unnecessarily.
- POS: search/barcode lookup, cash, manually confirmed terminal payment, simulated development adapter and future bank-approved terminal adapters behind a provider-independent interface. Atomic checkout-time stock reservations, finalize on payment, release on safe cancellation/expiry, hold on uncertain payment. Browser/PDF/email receipts; immutable historical amounts. Full/partial returns and refunds with approvals, restocking and audits.
- Supplier directory, purchase orders, partial receiving, inter-branch transfers (requested/dispatched/received), physical stock counts, discrepancy reports, authorized adjustments and immutable stock-movement audit records.
- Promotions: automatic, coupon, authorized manual discounts with scope, limits, stacking and audit. CRM: optional customer linkage for walk-in checkout, customer profiles, purchase history, segmentation, marketing consent/opt-out, configurable points/rewards/tiers and auditable loyalty adjustments on refunds.
- Notifications: registered recipients/roles/custom emails, category/severity, business and branch overrides, retries, deduplication and delivery logs.
- Advanced reporting: sales, inventory, gross margin, weighted-average COGS, demand forecasts with clearly labeled estimates, custom report builder, scheduled reports and CSV/XLSX exports; deliver incrementally.
- Orvexa is the **inventory authority** for Shopify, WooCommerce and Wix integrations; adapter-based synchronization, webhooks, idempotency, retries, conflict handling and oversell prevention.
- Subscription tiers Starter, Business, Enterprise with configurable entitlements, limits and trials; progressive active/grace/restricted-read-only/reactivated states. Even restricted tenants retain permitted exports, billing and reconciliation access.
- Separate, highly restricted Osyntra platform administration dashboard with MFA, tenant/subscription administration, diagnostics and auditable controlled support access.
- Email/password authentication, email verification/reset, secure password hashing, MFA for owners/platform administrators, rate limiting, session revocation and recovery. Tenant isolation, least privilege, audit logging and Jamaica Data Protection Act considerations.
- Encrypted automated backups, PostgreSQL WAL/PITR, offsite retention, restoration drills and RPO/RTO documentation. Central logs/metrics/alerts, secret redaction, suspicious login alerts, integration/payment/backup health.
- Self-service tenant exports and controlled closure: verified owner authorization, retention policy, legal holds, deletion workflow, status transparency and safe restoration windows where permitted.

### Scope and delivery discipline
Build an **operable vertical slice first**: sign in -> create/join business -> set up branch/tax -> import/create product -> receive stock -> checkout -> receipt -> sales/inventory reports. Defer nonessential integrations and predictive features until core stock/payment correctness is demonstrated. Distinguish MVP, post-MVP and integration-dependent items. Each stage must be runnable and must include focused tests. All money uses decimal arithmetic and explicit currency; timestamps are timezone-aware. Never trust client totals, roles, tenant IDs or stock quantities. Never store raw card details. Document environment setup, migrations and any manual provider configuration.

## Architectural baseline
Use the repository's existing Java/Spring Boot conventions. Prefer clear modules or feature packages for identity, tenancy, catalogue, inventory, purchasing, checkout, payments, tax, customers, loyalty, reporting, integrations, subscriptions and platform administration. PostgreSQL migrations should be versioned (reuse existing Flyway/Liquibase if present). Use database constraints and transaction boundaries for invariants. Expose versioned REST APIs and OpenAPI documentation where already used. Use pagination, filtering, stable errors, request validation, correlation IDs and idempotency keys for retryable operations. Design transactional outbox/queue processing for external side effects (emails, webhooks, notifications) to avoid sending them before database commits. Do not claim exactly-once delivery; enforce idempotent processing.

## Phase B1 — Identity, memberships, tenancy and invitations (MVP)
**Prompt:** Implement/extend users with one global identity and many business memberships. Each membership has exactly one predefined role and branch access. Add business creation, branch management, secure invitation issue/accept/revoke/expire flows (including existing users), active business selection, and backend authorization based on authenticated membership rather than client-supplied claims. Use strong password hashing, verification/reset, session expiry/revocation, rate limiting and owner MFA; scaffold extensible MFA for other users. Audit privilege changes. Separate platform staff identities/authorities from tenant memberships.
**Acceptance:** cross-tenant IDOR tests, branch denial tests, invitation replay/expiry tests, role boundary tests, account-with-two-businesses tests.

## Phase B2 — Settings, Jamaica tax engine and onboarding state (MVP)
**Prompt:** Add business settings with controlled branch overrides and persisted resumable onboarding checklist. Model tax profiles/rates/rules as effective-dated versions, supporting non-GCT-registered sellers, taxable/zero-rated/exempt items, tax-inclusive/exclusive prices where appropriate, per-product classification, rounding and immutable sale-time snapshots. Seed **illustrative** Jamaica profiles only with clear admin verification flags: general GCT is commonly 15% but legal eligibility and exceptions vary. Avoid automatic tax-law claims; support effective-date updates without rewriting past sales. Separate customs/landed cost from checkout tax. Document accountant/TAJ validation points.
**Acceptance:** tax test cases for exempt, zero-rated, taxable, unregistered and historical rate changes; no UI-entered tax total is trusted.

## Phase B3 — Catalogue, variants, units, barcode and imports (MVP)
**Prompt:** Implement simple products and variant parents, unique tenant-scoped SKU/barcode rules, variant-specific stock/price, global product low-stock threshold, category, tax profile and active status. Add explicit base-unit and purchase/sale-unit conversion factors with decimal precision and validation. Support existing UPC/EAN/GTIN strings and generated internal barcode namespace; never issue purported official GTINs. Add barcode SVG/PNG/PDF label generation with safe print dimensions. Build asynchronous or bounded CSV/XLSX import: downloadable template, header mapping, dry-run preview, row errors, duplicate policies, safe transaction chunks, idempotent retry and import job status. No arbitrary formulas or file execution; guard file size and spreadsheet formula injection in exports.
**Acceptance:** variant uniqueness, unit conversion, malformed file, duplicate SKU, import rollback/retry, and barcode collision tests.

## Phase B4 — Location inventory, costs, batches and purchasing (MVP)
**Prompt:** Model stock per location and variant, with available/reserved/on-hand quantities and immutable stock-movement ledger. Implement weighted-average inventory costing **per location** with decimal arithmetic; preserve acquisition cost on receipts and cost snapshots on sales. Define costing rules for negative stock (normally prohibit), returns, transfer-out/in and corrections. Add optional batch/expiry tracking and per-location batch quantities, expiry alerts and backend rejection of expired stock at sale. Build suppliers, purchase orders, partial goods receiving, purchasing unit conversions, adjustments with reason codes and physical stock counts/discrepancy approval. Add transfer requested/dispatched/received lifecycle and in-transit quantities. Ensure totals reconcile.
**Acceptance:** concurrent receipts/adjustments, partial PO receipts, transfer reconciliation, cost arithmetic and expired batch tests.

## Phase B5 — Checkout, reservations, payments, receipts and refunds (MVP)
**Prompt:** Implement server-priced POS basket/checkout with searchable SKU/barcode, branch-aware pricing, tax, controlled discounts and promotions. At checkout initiation atomically reserve quantities with expiry; do not reserve on mere cart add. Model sale, sale lines, payment attempts, provider state, reservation and receipt snapshots. Payment adapters: cash, manually confirmed external card terminal, development-only simulator; interface for future approved NCB/Scotiabank or other bank terminal integrations, subject to actual merchant/vendor SDK support. Do not pretend standalone terminals have APIs. Handle unknown/timeout state with reconciliation and no unsafe duplicate charge or reservation release. Commit stock and COGS once upon confirmed sale; generate immutable browser/PDF/email receipt payload. Support partial/full returns and refunds with reason, approval, restock decision, audit and manual external refund confirmation. Use idempotency keys and transactional safeguards.
**Acceptance:** parallel checkout for last unit, duplicate webhook/retry, unknown payment, refund arithmetic, tax snapshot and cancellation tests.

Build self-service export jobs for catalogue, inventory, customers, sales and permitted audit records; provide owner-authorized controlled closure workflow with retention/legal-hold checks and eventual deletion state.

## Phase B8 — E-commerce integrations (Post-MVP / provider dependent)
